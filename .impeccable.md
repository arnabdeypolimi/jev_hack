# Design Context — Push-Cube

- **Audience:** hackathon judges and a live crowd watching a projector; one volunteer plays with a mouse.
- **Use case:** a 60–90 s round, human vs TypeSafe's Jev, pushing a cube onto a goal. The crowd must read, from across a room, what Jev decided, how sure it was, and when it "thought harder".
- **Story to make visible:** System 1 (instinct: Jev's probabilities) vs System 2 (thinking harder: three simulated futures).
- **Tone:** a precise, playful lab bench — engineering drawing meets tabletop game. Confident and legible, not neon "AI".
- **Constraints:** projector in a lit room → light theme, high contrast, large type, no thin grey text; Three.js boards; no build step.
